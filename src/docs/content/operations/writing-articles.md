Anyone with an ampOS login can write an article for the company website. You write it, a publisher reviews it, and the publisher puts it on the site. You never need a developer.

## Start a new article

1. Click the **newspaper icon** in the top bar, next to Prayer Wall. This opens **Articles**.
2. Click **New article**.
3. Type a title at the top. The **Web address** on the right fills itself in from the title.

Click **Save draft** whenever you want. Nobody else sees a draft except you and the publishers.

## Write the body

Type in the big box. The toolbar above it has:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| ¶ | Normal paragraph |
| H2 / H3 | Heading and subheading. Use these to break up long articles. |
| **B** / *I* / U | Bold, italic, underline |
| Lists | Bullet or numbered list |
| Quote | Pulls a line out as a quote |
| Link | Highlight some words first, then click Link and paste the address |
| Image | Adds a picture where your cursor is |
| Line | A divider line |

The toolbar stays at the top of your screen as you scroll, so you can use it anywhere in a long article.

::: tip
Pasting from Word or Google Docs brings in the words only, not the fonts or colors. That is on purpose: the website uses its own look. Add headings and bold again with the toolbar after you paste.
:::

## Add pictures

Click in the text where the picture should go, then click the **Image** button. A popup opens with two halves:

- **Left:** drag a picture onto it, or click to choose one from your computer.
- **Right:** paste a link to a picture online. ampOS saves its own copy so it keeps working.

Then fill in:

- **Describe the image.** Required. Say what is in the picture, for example "Technician testing a breaker at a substation." This is read aloud to people who use screen readers and helps Google.
- **Caption.** Optional. Shows under the picture.

Pictures must be JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, under 10 MB.

::: note
Some websites block copying their pictures. If a link does not work, save the picture to your computer and drag it onto the left half instead. Only use pictures you have the right to use.
:::

## Fill in the right-hand panel

- **Web address.** The end of the article's link, like `why-we-test-breakers`. Lowercase words with dashes. You can edit it until the article is published. After that it is locked so links never break.
- **Summary.** One or two sentences, about 160 characters. Shows on the article list and in Google results. The counter turns orange when you go over.
- **Cover image.** Optional. The big picture at the top of the article and on its card in the list. Add a description for it too.
- **Category** and **Tags.** Optional. Separate tags with commas.

The **Author** is you. A publisher can change it if you are writing for someone else.

## Send it for review

When it is ready, click **Send for review**. To send it, you need:

- a title
- a web address
- a summary
- some text in the body
- a description for the cover image, if you added one

If anything is missing, ampOS tells you what.

While it is in review you can still make edits. If you want to pull it back, click **Move back to draft**.

## What happens next

A publisher will do one of two things:

- **Publish it.** It goes live on the website a few minutes later. The status changes to **Published** and a **View on site** link appears.
- **Send it back.** It returns to **Draft** with a yellow note at the top explaining what to change. Make the changes and click **Send for review** again.

::: warning
Once an article is published, only a publisher can change it. If you spot a mistake after it goes live, ask a publisher to fix it or to unpublish it so you can edit.
:::

## Finding your articles

On the Articles page, click **Mine** to see everything you have written. Status shows as **Draft**, **In review**, **Published** or **Archived**.

To delete one of your drafts, open it and click **Delete** at the bottom of the right-hand panel. You cannot delete an article once it is published.

## Who are the publishers?

Admins, plus anyone an Admin has added to the publishers list. Ask your manager if you are not sure who to nudge about a review.

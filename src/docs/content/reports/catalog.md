Over 60 forms ship with ampOS. This page groups them so you can find the one you need.

## Picking the right form

Three questions, in order:

1. **What is the equipment?** Transformer, breaker, cable, switchgear, switch, CT/PT, relay.
2. **Acceptance or maintenance?** ATS for new equipment being energized the first time. MTS for existing equipment on a periodic cycle.
3. **What variety?** Dry type or liquid filled. Electronic trip or thermal-magnetic. Low, medium, or high voltage.

::: note
`ATS 25` in a form name refers to the 2025 edition of the acceptance testing specifications. Where both an older and an ATS 25 form exist, use ATS 25 for new work unless the customer's specification calls out the older edition.
:::

## Nothing fits?

Build your own. The custom form builder creates report templates without code, and they behave like built-in reports, with the same review flow and the same deliverables. See [Custom forms](/docs/custom-forms/overview).

If a NETA form is genuinely missing from this list, tell an administrator. Built-in forms get added.

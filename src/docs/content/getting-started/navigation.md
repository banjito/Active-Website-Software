ampOS is big. This page is a map.

## The portal

The portal at `/portal` is home. It shows a tile for every area you have access to: Jobs, Sales, HR, Lab, Office, Admin, and one tile per division.

Click the logo in the top-left corner of any page to come back here.

## The sidebar

Inside a portal, the left sidebar lists that portal's pages. It collapses on narrow screens and on tablets; tap the menu button to open it.

The sidebar is scoped to where you are. If you are in the Sales portal, you see sales pages. If you are in a division dashboard, you see that division's jobs, sites, customers, and scheduling.

## The top bar

The bar across the top of every page carries:

- **Back arrow.** Returns to the previous page. On a report, it returns you to the job that report belongs to, which is usually what you want.
- **Search.** Searches jobs, customers, and contacts.
- **Shortcuts.** Your saved quick links. See below.
- **Notifications.** The bell. Reports waiting on you, jobs assigned to you, announcements.
- **Theme toggle.** Light and dark. ampOS remembers your choice per device.
- **Your avatar.** Profile, settings, and sign out.

## Shortcuts

The shortcuts menu is a personal set of links to the pages you actually use. Rather than clicking through three levels of menu to reach the same page fifteen times a day, pin it once.

To set them up, open the shortcuts dropdown and choose **Manage**. Pick a portal on the left, then the pages you want from that portal. Drag to reorder. Your shortcuts follow your account, not the device.

::: tip
There are separate shortcut lists for review queues and issues, so reviewers can jump straight to what needs attention without touching their main list.
:::

## Moving between divisions

If you work across divisions, use the division switcher in the sidebar. Switching changes the default filter on jobs, sites, customers, and scheduling. It does not hide data you have permission to see. To see everything at once, use **All jobs** instead of a division's jobs page.

## Getting back to a job

This is the most common navigation problem in the app: you are eleven fields deep in a transformer report and you need to check the scope on the job.

The **back to job** button in the top bar takes you straight there from any report. It is a real button, not the browser back arrow, so nothing you typed is lost.

::: warning
Use the in-app back button, not your browser's. The browser back button works, but on a long report it can drop you at the top of the page instead of where you were reading.
:::

## URLs are stable

Every page in ampOS has a real address, so you can bookmark it or paste it in a message.

| To share | Copy the URL from |
|---|---|
| A specific job | The job page |
| One test report | The report page |
| A customer record | The customer page |
| A filtered job list | The jobs list after you set the filters |

Job list filters are stored in the URL, so a link to "all in-progress Field Tech jobs" opens exactly that for whoever you send it to.

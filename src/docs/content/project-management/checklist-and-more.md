Besides the jobs list, the [PM dashboard](/docs/project-management/overview) has tabs for the work around the jobs.

## Checklist

Every job gets the same setup steps, like contract signed, customer contacted, COI issued, SOV created, and mobilization billed. The **Checklist** tab shows every job's steps in one grid.

- Each step has a due date counted from the contract date. Late steps flag the job on the dashboard.
- Only PMs tick steps. Everyone else can see them.
- Some steps tick themselves. **SOV created** ticks when the job's SOV is saved.

## Temp hours

For jobs using temp workers from an agency.

1. Under **Agencies and temps**, add your temp agency, who gets its weekly email, and the temps who work for you. You only do this once.
2. Each week, enter each temp's hours by job.
3. Click **Mark complete and send**. ampOS sends one email with the week's totals. **Send again** resends it if something changed.

Temp hours also count in the job's **Hours used**.

## Change Orders

Every Change Order on your jobs, split into **Waiting for approval** and **Approved**, with its price and letter. Click one to open it.

How Change Orders are written, sent for signature, and approved is covered in [Change orders](/docs/jobs/change-orders). Once approved, they join the job's [schedule of values](/docs/project-management/schedule-of-values).

## Crew

Who is clocked in on your jobs right now, and each person's hours this week. You see names and hours on your own jobs only, never pay.

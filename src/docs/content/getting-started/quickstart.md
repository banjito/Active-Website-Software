This page takes you from "I have an account" to "I finished something." It assumes nothing.

## Before you start

You need an invite from an administrator. ampOS does not have open sign-up; your email address has to be on the approved list before you can get in. If you cannot sign in, that is almost always why. Ask whoever runs the system at your company to add you.

## Step 1: Sign in

1. Go to your company's ampOS address in a browser. Chrome and Edge work best.
2. Enter your work email and password. If you were invited but never set a password, use **Forgot password** to set one.
3. The first time you sign in, you will be sent to **Profile setup**. Fill in your name, division, and phone number. This is what shows up on reports you sign, so spell it the way you want customers to see it.

::: note
ampOS keeps you signed in on a device until you sign out. On a shared field tablet, sign out when you are done.
:::

## Step 2: Land on the portal

After sign-in you land on the **portal**, a grid of tiles, one per area of the business. Which tiles you see depends on your role. A field tech sees jobs and reports. An office admin sees a lot more.

The portal is home base. If you ever get lost, click the logo in the top left to come back here.

## Step 3: Find your job

Click the tile for your division, or go to **Jobs** to see everything.

The jobs list is filterable. The three filters you will use constantly:

- **Status.** Narrow to `In progress` to see live work.
- **Division.** Narrow to your own crew.
- **Search.** Type a job number, a customer name, or part of the job title.

Click a job to open it.

## Step 4: Open the job

The job page has tabs across the top. For your first pass, only two matter:

- **Overview.** Who the customer is, where the site is, what the scope says, and when it is due.
- **Assets.** The list of equipment on this job, and the report attached to each one.

Read [The job page](/docs/jobs/the-job-page) when you want the full tour.

## Step 5: Create a report

1. Go to the **Assets** tab.
2. Click **Add asset** and give it a name that matches the nameplate, for example `Main Switchgear SWG-1`.
3. Pick the report type that matches what you tested. If you are not sure which one, check the [report catalog](/docs/reports/catalog).
4. The report form opens. Fill in the job information at the top. Most of it is already filled in from the job.
5. Work down the form. It saves as you go.

## Step 6: Submit it for review

When the report is finished, click **Submit for review** at the top of the form. That moves it from `In progress` to `Ready for review` and puts it in the reviewer's queue.

You are done. Someone else approves it from here.

::: warning
Submitting for review locks most fields. If you realize you made a mistake, ask a reviewer to send it back; they can move it to `In progress` again.
:::

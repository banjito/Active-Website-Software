The community board is where internal news goes: company updates, shout-outs, and things that do not warrant an email to everybody.

## Where it is

**Community** in the sidebar. There is also a popover in the top bar showing recent posts, so new items surface without anyone going looking.

## Posting

1. Open the community board.
2. Click **New post**.
3. Give it a title and write the content.
4. Pick a category.
5. Pin it if it needs to stay at the top.
6. Publish.

## What belongs here

- Company news: new contracts, new hires, milestones
- Recognition, like a crew that pulled off a difficult job
- Reminders that are useful but not urgent
- Policy notes that do not require a formal acknowledgment

## What does not

| Instead of posting… | Use |
|---|---|
| Something requiring formal acknowledgment | [HR compliance](/docs/hr/compliance) |
| Something specific to one job | [Job notes](/docs/jobs/files-and-documents) |
| Something urgent and safety-critical | A phone call. Then post it. |
| A formal HR announcement to a targeted group | [HR announcements](/docs/hr/announcements) |

::: warning
Never rely on the community board for anything time-critical or safety-related. It is a noticeboard. People read it when they read it.
:::

## Pinning

Pinned posts stay at the top. Use it sparingly. A board with six pinned posts has none, because people stop distinguishing them.

Unpin when it stops being current. Stale pins are how a board becomes something people scroll past.

## Who sees it

Everyone with an ampOS account. It is not customer-facing, and it is not targeted. For a message aimed at specific people, use HR announcements instead.

## Making it worth reading

Boards die quietly. What keeps one alive:

- **A regular poster.** Someone whose job includes putting something up weekly.
- **Recognition, not just information.** The posts people actually read are about people.
- **Brevity.** Three sentences and a photo beats four paragraphs.

If nobody has posted in a month, it is not a tool anyone will check when you need them to.

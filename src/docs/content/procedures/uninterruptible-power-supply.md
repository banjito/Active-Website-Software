## Purpose

Provide guidelines and procedures for testing the UPS to meet the NETA ATS (Acceptance
Testing Specifications) standards, so the UPS is tested thoroughly and meets the specified
performance criteria.

## Scope

Testing of the uninterruptible power supply in accordance with the NETA ATS standards. It
covers the steps and precautions to be followed by qualified personnel to ensure accurate and
reliable testing.

## Safety and precautions

::: danger
Verify that the UPS is de-energized and properly isolated before initiating any testing
procedures, and implement proper lockout/tagout to prevent accidental energization. A UPS
holds stored energy: confirm the battery string is isolated and the DC bus is discharged
before working inside the cabinet.
:::

- Ensure that all necessary safety measures and precautions are followed during the testing
  process.
- Adhere to all relevant safety regulations and wear appropriate personal protective
  equipment (PPE) during the testing process.

## Equipment and tools

- Insulation-resistance tester
- Micro-ohmmeter
- Multimeter
- Safety barriers and signs
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)

## Procedure

::: note
Ensure that all testing equipment is properly calibrated and maintained.
:::

### 1. Preparation

1. Review the uninterruptible power supply specifications, drawings, and nameplate details.
2. Verify that the testing equipment is in good working condition and properly calibrated.
3. Assemble all necessary tools, equipment, and PPE required for testing.
4. Ensure that the testing area is clear of any potential hazards and properly marked with
   safety barriers and signs.

### 2. Visual inspection

1. Inspect the UPS for any physical damage, loose connections, or signs of overheating.
2. Check the condition of the busbars and other components of the cabinet.

### 3. Insulation-resistance test

1. Use an insulation-resistance tester to measure the insulation resistance of the busbars
   inside the UPS.
2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and record the insulation-resistance readings for
   each phase.
3. Compare the measured insulation-resistance values with the specified minimum values as per
   NETA ATS standards. Note any deviations for further investigation.

### 4. Contact-resistance test

1. Use a micro-ohmmeter to measure the contact resistance of bolted connections.
2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and measure the contact resistance of each phase.
3. Record the contact resistance values and compare them with the manufacturer's
   specifications or the expected values as per NETA ATS standards.

## Conclusion

1. Clean work area.
2. Remove safety barriers and signs.
3. Remove LOTO.

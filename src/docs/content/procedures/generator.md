## Purpose

Provide guidelines and procedures for testing low-voltage switchgear to meet the NETA ATS
(Acceptance Testing Specifications) standards, so the switchgear is tested thoroughly and
meets the specified performance criteria.

## Scope

Testing of low-voltage switchgear in accordance with the NETA ATS standards. It covers the
steps and precautions to be followed by qualified personnel to ensure accurate and reliable
testing.

## Safety and precautions

::: danger
Verify that the equipment is de-energized and properly isolated before initiating any testing
procedures, and implement proper lockout/tagout to prevent accidental energization. On a
generator, that includes disabling the automatic start signal and isolating the starting
battery.
:::

- Ensure that all necessary safety measures and precautions are followed during the testing
  process.
- Adhere to all relevant safety regulations and wear appropriate personal protective
  equipment (PPE) during the testing process.

## Equipment and tools

- Insulation-resistance tester
- Micro-ohmmeter
- Primary injection test set
- Relay test set
- Multimeter
- Test leads and cables
- Safety barriers and signs
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)

## Procedure

::: note
Ensure that all testing equipment is properly calibrated and maintained.
:::

### 1. Preparation

1. Review the specifications, drawings, and nameplate details.
2. Verify that the testing equipment is in good working condition and properly calibrated.
3. Assemble all necessary tools, equipment, and PPE required for testing.
4. Ensure that the testing area is clear of any potential hazards and properly marked with
   safety barriers and signs.

### 2. Visual inspection

1. Inspect the equipment for any physical damage, loose connections, or signs of overheating.
2. Check the condition of circuit breakers, contacts, busbars, and other components.
3. Verify that all safety interlocks and mechanisms are in proper working condition.

### 3. Insulation-resistance test

1. Use an insulation-resistance tester to measure the insulation resistance.
2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and record the insulation-resistance readings for
   each phase.
3. Compare the measured insulation-resistance values with the specified minimum values as per
   NETA ATS standards. Note any deviations for further investigation.

### 4. Contact-resistance test

1. Use a micro-ohmmeter to measure the contact resistance of circuit breakers and busbars.
2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and measure the contact resistance of each phase.
3. Record the contact resistance values and compare them with the manufacturer's
   specifications or the expected values as per NETA ATS standards.

### 5. Overcurrent relay testing

1. Use a relay test set to test the operation of overcurrent relays.
2. Connect the test set to the relay's secondary side and simulate various fault conditions.
3. Verify that the overcurrent relays operate correctly and trip the associated circuit
   breakers within the specified time.

### 6. Functional testing

1. Conduct functional tests for all control and protection devices.
2. Verify the correct operation of circuit breakers, relays, motor starters, interlocks, and
   other auxiliary devices.
3. Ensure that all control functions, including remote operation and indication, are
   functioning as intended.

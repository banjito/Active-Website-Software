A signature profile is a saved signature block: name, title, email, phone, and which section it belongs to. Create it once, pick it from a list forever after.

## Why they exist

Before profiles, every executive summary meant retyping the same four names and phone numbers. Retyping produces typos, and a typo in a phone number on a customer document is a call you never receive.

## What a profile holds

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The person as they should appear on a customer document |
| **Title** | `Senior Field Engineer`, `Project Manager` |
| **Email** | Work address |
| **Phone** | The number a customer should actually call |
| **Section** | Which signature block this profile is for |

## Sections

Documents have several signature slots, and profiles are tagged with which one they belong to:

- **Work performed by.** The technicians who did the testing.
- **Reviewed by.** Whoever approved the reports.
- **Project manager.** The point of contact.

Tagging by section means when you are filling the "reviewed by" block, you are offered reviewers rather than the whole company.

## Creating one

Open the signature profile manager from a document that uses signatures, or from your profile settings. Enter the details, pick the section, save.

Profiles can be edited and deleted as people change roles or leave.

## Using them

When generating an executive summary or another signed document, select the profiles you want. Several at once is normal: two technicians under "work performed by," one reviewer, one project manager.

The details drop into the document automatically.

## Keeping them current

::: warning
A profile with an old title or a disconnected phone number goes onto customer documents exactly as written. When someone changes roles or leaves, update or remove their profile. Nobody notices this until a customer calls a dead number.
:::

## Profiles versus your account profile

Two different things:

- **Your account profile** is your login identity, the name attached to reports you write and approve. See [Accounts and roles](/docs/getting-started/accounts-and-roles).
- **A signature profile** is a reusable block of text for customer documents.

They usually contain the same name. They are not the same record, and updating one does not update the other.

## Who should have one

Anyone whose name appears on customer documents: field technicians who sign as work performed by, reviewers, and project managers.

Anyone whose name never appears does not need one.

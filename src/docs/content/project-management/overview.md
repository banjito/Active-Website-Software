The PM dashboard is where a project manager runs their jobs from week to week. Open it with **[My Jobs](/pm-dashboard)** in the Field Tech menu.

## Who sees it

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| Admins, Operations Managers, Project Managers, and division leads | Every active job, at any location |
| Anyone named as a job's PM | Only the jobs where they are the PM |

To make someone a job's PM, set the **Project Manager** on the job. Every job should have one. A job with no PM shows up with a "No lead" flag.

## The jobs list

The dashboard opens on **Jobs**. Use **Which jobs** to switch between your own jobs and everything at a location. The month picker sets which month the money columns show.

Only active jobs are listed: pending, in progress, progress billing, ready to bill, and on hold. Finished, billed, and cancelled jobs drop off.

### Needs attention

Jobs that need a look go to the top, each with a flag saying why:

| Flag | What it means |
|---|---|
| Late step | A checklist step is past its due date |
| Change Order pending | A Change Order is waiting for the customer |
| Ready to bill | The job is marked ready for accounting |
| SOV not updated | Last week's update hasn't been entered |
| No lead | The job has no PM |

Click a job to open its page. That's where the [schedule of values](/docs/project-management/schedule-of-values), [weekly updates](/docs/project-management/weekly-updates), and [billing](/docs/project-management/billing-from-the-sov) live.

## The other tabs

- **Checklist.** The setup steps for every job in one grid. See [Checklist, temp hours, and Change Orders](/docs/project-management/checklist-and-more).
- **Crew.** Who is clocked in on your jobs, and their hours this week.
- **Temp hours.** Each week's hours for temp workers, sent to the agency in one email.
- **Change Orders.** Every Change Order on your jobs and where each one stands.

::: note
Hours come from the time clock. Until crews clock in through ampOS, **Hours used** and the **Crew** tab stay near empty.
:::

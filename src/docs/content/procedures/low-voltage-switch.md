## Purpose

Provide guidelines and procedures for testing low-voltage switches and disconnects to meet
the NETA ATS (Acceptance Testing Specifications) standards, so the low-voltage switches are
tested thoroughly and meet the specified performance criteria.

## Scope

Testing of low-voltage switches in accordance with the NETA ATS standards. It covers the
steps and precautions to be followed by qualified personnel to ensure accurate and reliable
testing.

## Safety and precautions

::: danger
Verify that the switch is de-energized and properly isolated before initiating any testing
procedures, and implement proper lockout/tagout to prevent accidental energization.
:::

- Ensure that all necessary safety measures and precautions are followed during the testing
  process.
- Adhere to all relevant safety regulations and wear appropriate personal protective
  equipment (PPE) during the testing process.

## Equipment and tools

- Insulation-resistance tester
- Digital low-resistance ohmmeter
- Multimeter
- Test leads and cables
- Safety barriers and signs
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)

## Procedure

::: note
Ensure that all testing equipment is properly calibrated and maintained.
:::

### 1. Preparation

1. Review the low-voltage switch specifications, drawings, and nameplate details.
2. Verify that the testing equipment is in good working condition and properly calibrated.
3. Assemble all necessary tools, equipment, and PPE required for testing.
4. Ensure that the testing area is clear of any potential hazards and properly marked with
   safety barriers and signs.

### 2. Visual inspection

1. Inspect the low-voltage switch for any physical damage, loose connections, or signs of
   overheating.
2. Verify that all safety interlocks and mechanisms are in proper working condition.

### 3. Insulation-resistance test

1. Use an insulation-resistance tester to measure the insulation resistance of the
   switch/disconnect.
2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and record the insulation-resistance readings for
   each phase.
3. Compare the measured insulation-resistance values with the specified minimum values as per
   NETA ATS standards. Note any deviations for further investigation.

### 4. Contact-resistance test

1. Use a micro-ohmmeter to measure the contact resistance through bolted connections. Measure
   contact resistance across each switchblade and fuseholder.
2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and measure the contact resistance of each phase.
3. Record the contact resistance values and compare them with the manufacturer's
   specifications or the expected values as per NETA ATS standards.

### 5. Functional testing

1. Compare bolted connection resistance values to values of similar connections. Investigate
   values which deviate from those of similar bolted connections by more than 50 percent of
   the lowest value (**7.5.1.1.A.8.1**).
2. Verify the correct manual operation of the low-voltage switch.
3. Ensure that all control functions, including remote operation and indication, are
   functioning as intended.

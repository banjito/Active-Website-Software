A territory is a slice of the business, usually geographic, with sales reps assigned to it and a revenue target attached.

## What a territory holds

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Name** | `North Alabama`, `Gulf Coast Industrial` |
| **Region** | The broader area it sits in |
| **Manager** | Who owns the number |
| **Assigned reps** | Who works it |
| **Revenue target** | What it should produce this period |
| **Current revenue** | What it has produced |
| **Accounts** | How many customers it covers |
| **Opportunities** | How many are open in it |

## Creating one

1. Go to **Territories** and click **New territory**.
2. Name it, set the region, pick the manager.
3. Set the revenue target for the period.
4. Save, then assign reps.

## Assigning sales reps

Open the territory and use **Manage sales reps**. Add or remove people.

Several reps can share a territory. One rep can cover several territories. Both are normal on a small team.

::: warning
Every customer should fall inside exactly one territory. Customers in no territory drop out of territory reporting entirely. The revenue is real but it appears nowhere, and the totals quietly stop adding up.
:::

## The territory map

The map view shows coverage geographically. Use it to spot the two problems territory lists hide:

- **Gaps.** An area with customers and no rep.
- **Overlap.** Two reps calling on the same plant, which the customer notices before you do.

## Performance

Each territory has a performance view: revenue against target, open pipeline, win rate, account count.

Compare territories by **rate**, not by total. A small territory hitting 130% of a modest target is performing better than a large one at 80%, and only the percentage tells you that.

## Account ownership

Territories decide which rep owns which customer. When a customer moves territory, whether you reorganize or they open a plant elsewhere, reassign the account explicitly rather than assuming it follows.

## When to reorganize

Signs a territory needs splitting:

- One rep cannot physically call on everyone
- The target keeps being missed while pipeline stays healthy
- Response times to new inquiries are slipping

Signs two should merge:

- Both are consistently under target
- Reps are travelling past each other's customers to reach their own

## Territories and divisions

Different things. A **territory** is a sales area. A **division** is an operational crew. A territory's work can be delivered by several divisions, and often is.

See [Portals and divisions](/docs/getting-started/portals-and-divisions).

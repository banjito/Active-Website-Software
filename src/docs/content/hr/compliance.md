Compliance is mostly about proof: that an employee received and accepted a policy, that a signature is valid, and that you can produce the reports regulators ask for.

## Document acknowledgment

When an employee has to formally accept a document, such as a policy, a handbook, or a safety procedure, use acknowledgment rather than an announcement.

1. Go to **HR → Compliance → Document acknowledgment**.
2. Select the document.
3. Choose who must acknowledge it.
4. Send.

Employees receive it, read it, and acknowledge. The system records who acknowledged what, and when.

::: warning
An announcement is not an acknowledgment. It proves you sent something, not that anyone accepted it. For anything you might need to rely on, like safety policies, handbooks, and codes of conduct, use acknowledgment.
:::

### Chasing outstanding acknowledgments

The outstanding list is the point of the feature. A policy 80% acknowledged is a policy that does not cover the 20% who did not.

Chase them. Then chase them again.

## E-sign recordkeeping

Every electronically signed document is retained with its audit trail: who signed, when, and from where.

That trail is what makes an electronic signature defensible. A signed PDF with no record of who signed it is a picture of a signature.

### What is retained

- The signed document
- Signer identity
- Timestamp
- The version signed

Version matters. When a policy is reissued and re-signed, you need to know which version each person accepted. See [Employee profiles](/docs/hr/employee-profiles) for how document versioning works.

## EEO reporting

Generate the equal employment opportunity reports required of you.

Accuracy depends entirely on employee records being complete. Incomplete demographic data produces a report that is wrong, and the time to fix that is before the report is due.

## Retention

Different records have different required retention periods, and they vary by jurisdiction. Know yours.

The general principle in ampOS applies here too: **deactivate, do not delete**. A deleted record cannot be produced when it is asked for, and departure is exactly when records become most likely to be needed.

See [Data backup](/docs/admin/data-backup).

## Access

Compliance data is among the most restricted in the system. Confirm who has access deliberately rather than assuming a role is approximately right.

See [Roles and permissions](/docs/admin/roles-and-permissions).

## A quarterly check

1. Any outstanding acknowledgments?
2. Any expired certifications on active employees?
3. Are employee records complete enough for the reports you owe?
4. Have any policies changed without being reissued for acknowledgment?

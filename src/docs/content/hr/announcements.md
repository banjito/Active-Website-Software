Announcements push a message to everyone, or to a chosen list of employees.

## Creating one

1. Go to **HR → Announcements**.
2. Click **New announcement**.
3. Write the title and content.
4. Choose the **audience**.
5. Publish.

## Targeting an audience

Two options:

- **Everyone.** Leave the audience unset. It goes to all employees.
- **Selected people.** Pick specific employees.

::: tip
Target narrowly. An announcement that goes to everyone when it applies to twelve people is how announcements stop being read. Once people learn that most of these do not concern them, the one that does gets skipped too.
:::

The same targeting model is used for e-sign form assignment, so the mental model carries across the HR portal.

## What belongs in an announcement

- Policy changes
- Benefits enrollment periods and deadlines
- Schedule changes affecting a group
- Organizational changes
- Anything with a date attached that people need to act on

## What does not

| Instead of an announcement | Use |
|---|---|
| Something needing signed acknowledgment | [Compliance](/docs/hr/compliance) |
| Company news, recognition, general notices | [Community board](/docs/operations/community-board) |
| Something about one job | [Job notes](/docs/jobs/files-and-documents) |
| Something urgent and safety-critical | A phone call. Then announce it. |

::: warning
An announcement is not proof anyone read it. If you need evidence that people received and accepted a policy, use document acknowledgment. It captures a signature and a timestamp. See [Compliance](/docs/hr/compliance).
:::

## Writing one people will read

- **Say the point in the title.** `Benefits enrollment closes 30 November`, not `Important update`.
- **Lead with the action.** What the reader has to do, and by when.
- **Keep it short.** Three sentences beats three paragraphs.
- **Say who to ask.** A name.

## Where employees see them

In the HR portal and in the employee self-service portal.

For anything genuinely time-critical, do not rely on people logging in. Announce it *and* tell them.

## Editing and removing

Announcements can be edited and removed.

If you are correcting something material, post a correction rather than quietly editing. Some people have already read the original, and they will act on what they read.

## Purpose

Provide guidelines and procedures for testing medium-voltage cables to meet the NETA ATS
(Acceptance Testing Specifications) standards, so the medium-voltage cables are tested
thoroughly and meet the specified performance criteria.

## Scope

Testing of medium-voltage cables in accordance with the NETA ATS standards. It covers the
steps and precautions to be followed by qualified personnel to ensure accurate and reliable
testing.

## Safety and precautions

::: danger
Verify that the cables are de-energized and properly isolated before initiating any testing
procedures, and implement proper lockout/tagout to prevent accidental energization. Both ends
of the cable must be under positive control for the duration of the test, and the cable must
be grounded and discharged after every high-voltage application.
:::

- Ensure that all necessary safety measures and precautions are followed during the testing
  process.
- Adhere to all relevant safety regulations and wear appropriate personal protective
  equipment (PPE) during the testing process.

## Equipment and tools

- VLF (very low frequency) hipot
- Insulation-resistance tester
- Megger or high-voltage insulation tester
- Multimeter
- Test leads and cables
- Safety barriers and signs
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)

## Procedure

::: note
Ensure that all testing equipment is properly calibrated and maintained.
:::

### 1. Preparation

1. Review the medium-voltage cable specifications, drawings, and labeling details.
2. Verify that the testing equipment is in good working condition and properly calibrated.
3. Assemble all necessary tools, equipment, and PPE required for testing.
4. Ensure that the testing area is clear of any potential hazards and properly marked with
   safety barriers and signs.

### 2. Visual inspection

1. Perform a visual and mechanical inspection in accordance with NETA ATS **7.3.3.A**.

### 3. Electrical tests

1. Perform resistance measurements through bolted connections with a low-resistance ohmmeter
   in accordance with Section **7.3.3.A.3.1**.
2. Perform an insulation-resistance test individually on each conductor and each shield with
   all other conductors and shields grounded. Apply voltage in accordance with manufacturer's
   published data. In the absence of manufacturer's data, use Table 100.1.
3. Perform a shield-continuity test on each power cable by ohmmeter method.
4. Perform a dielectric withstand test.
5. Perform power factor/dissipation factor (tan delta).
6. Record and compare the measured values with NETA ATS or manufacturer standards. Note any
   deviations for further investigation.

## Conclusion

1. Clean work area.
2. Remove safety barriers and signs.
3. Remove LOTO.

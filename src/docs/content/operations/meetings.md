## What happened

ampOS used to include a built-in Level 10 meeting system. That has been replaced by Runway, a dedicated tool. Clicking **Meetings** in ampOS, from the portal, the sidebar, or a shortcut, opens Runway in a new tab.

You will need a Runway account. Ask an administrator if you do not have one.

## The format

Runway runs the Level 10 format, which is worth understanding whichever tool holds it. A weekly meeting, same time, same agenda, ninety minutes:

1. **Check-in.** Brief personal and professional good news.
2. **Scorecard.** The handful of numbers you watch weekly. On or off track, no discussion.
3. **Rocks.** The quarterly priorities. On or off track.
4. **Customer and employee headlines.** Anything anyone needs to know.
5. **To-do review.** Last week's commitments. Done or not done.
6. **Issues.** The bulk of the meeting. Identify, discuss, solve.
7. **Wrap.** New to-dos, who is telling whom what.

## Why the format works

The first five sections are deliberately fast. Reporting on-track or off-track takes ten seconds; the discussion happens in the issues list, not in the reporting.

Meetings that run long almost always do so because someone started solving a problem during the scorecard.

| Open purchase orders | The weekly PO report |

:::

## Issues, not complaints

An issue is something with a decision attached. "We keep getting to site without gate access" is an issue. "The customer is difficult" is a complaint.

The test: at the end of the discussion, is there something someone will do, by a date? If not, it was not an issue.

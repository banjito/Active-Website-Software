## Purpose

Provide guidelines and procedures for testing the transfer control panel to meet the NETA ATS
(Acceptance Testing Specifications) standards, so the control panel is tested thoroughly and
meets the specified performance criteria.

## Scope

Testing of transfer switches in accordance with the NETA ATS standards. It covers the steps
and precautions to be followed by qualified personnel to ensure accurate and reliable
testing.

::: tip
This procedure covers the control panel. The switching mechanism itself is covered by the
[transfer switch MOP](/docs/procedures/transfer-switch).
:::

## Safety and precautions

::: danger
Verify that the control panel is de-energized and properly isolated before initiating any
testing procedures, and implement proper lockout/tagout to prevent accidental energization.
:::

- Ensure that all necessary safety measures and precautions are followed during the testing
  process.
- Adhere to all relevant safety regulations and wear appropriate personal protective
  equipment (PPE) during the testing process.

## Equipment and tools

- Digital low-resistance ohmmeter
- Insulation-resistance tester
- Multimeter
- Relay test set
- Test leads and cables
- Safety barriers and signs
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)

## Procedure

::: note
Ensure that all testing equipment is properly calibrated and maintained.
:::

### 1. Preparation

1. Review the specifications, drawings, and nameplate details.
2. Verify that the testing equipment is in good working condition and properly calibrated.
3. Assemble all necessary tools, equipment, and PPE required for testing.
4. Ensure that the testing area is clear of any potential hazards and properly marked with
   safety barriers and signs.

### 2. Visual inspection

1. Perform a visual and mechanical inspection in accordance with NETA ATS **7.22.3.A.2**.

### 3. Electrical tests

1. Perform resistance measurements through bolted connections with a low-resistance ohmmeter
   in accordance with Section **7.22.3.A.8.1**.
2. Perform insulation-resistance tests for one minute on each pole, phase-to-phase and
   phase-to-ground, with the switch in both source positions and across each open pole. Test
   voltage shall be in accordance with manufacturer's published data. In the absence of
   manufacturer's published data, use Table 100.1.
3. Perform a contact/pole-resistance test.
4. Verify settings and operation of control devices.
5. Calibrate and set all relays and timers in accordance with Section **7.9**.
6. Compare bolted connection resistance values to values of similar connections. Investigate
   values which deviate from those of similar bolted connections by more than 50 percent of
   the lowest value (**7.22.3.A.8.1**).
7. Record and compare the measured values with NETA ATS or manufacturer standards. Note any
   deviations for further investigation.

## Conclusion

1. Clean work area.
2. Remove safety barriers and signs.
3. Remove LOTO.

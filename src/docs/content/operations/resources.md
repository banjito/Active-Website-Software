Resources are the things there are not enough of: the high-current test set, the bucket truck, the one technician certified on relays.

Booking them centrally is how two crews stop showing up for the same equipment on the same morning.

## The resource list

**Resources** in the sidebar, or your division's resources page.

Each resource shows what it is, whether it is available, and who has it booked.

## Booking one

Resources are allocated from the job:

1. Open the job and go to **Tracking**.
2. In resource allocation, click **Add**.
3. Pick the resource, the dates, and its role on the job.
4. Save.

## Allocation statuses

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Planned** | Pencilled in |
| **Scheduled** | On the calendar |
| **Confirmed** | The person or asset is committed |
| **In progress** | In use now |
| **Completed** | Done |
| **Cancelled** | Released |

::: tip
The gap between `Planned` and `Confirmed` is the whole value of this feature. A schedule where everything is "planned" tells you nothing about what will actually happen on Tuesday.
:::

## Conflicts

If a resource is already booked for overlapping dates, ampOS shows you the conflict rather than blocking the booking.

That is deliberate, since sometimes a half-day each is exactly what you meant. But it does mean somebody has to look. Check the calendar before assuming a booking is clean.

## Releasing a resource

Mark the allocation **Completed** or **Cancelled** when you are done.

A job that finished three weeks ago still holding the only VLF set is why the next crew thinks it is unavailable and rents one.

## People as resources

Technicians are resources too. Their allocations feed the scheduling calendar, and approved time off from HR shows there as unavailable, so you do not schedule around a vacation you forgot about.

See [Scheduling](/docs/jobs/scheduling) and [Time and attendance](/docs/hr/time-and-attendance).

## Resources versus field equipment

Related but different:

- **Resources** are about *booking*: who has it, when.
- **Field equipment** is about *tracking*: where it is, what condition it is in, when it is due for service.

A test set appears in both. See [Field equipment and vehicles](/docs/operations/field-equipment).

## Making this work

The system only helps if bookings are real. Two habits:

1. Book before you commit to a customer date, not after.
2. Release what you are not using, the day you stop using it.

Without both, people stop trusting the list and go back to asking around, which is where you started.

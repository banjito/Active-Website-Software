Once a week, the PM records how much of each SOV line got done. Those numbers add up into the month's bill, so a missed week means a short or late invoice.

Open the job and pick the **Weekly update** tab. It opens on the week that's due.

## Filling in a week

1. Use **Previous week** and **Next week** to pick the week.
2. Type what got done on each line this week.
3. Click **Save draft** to keep it without locking it.
4. Click **Approve week** when it's right.

Each week shows a status: **Not started**, **Draft**, **Due Monday**, or **Approved**. Last week is due Monday. If it isn't approved, the job gets an "SOV not updated" flag on the dashboard.

## Counts filled in from reports

If a line is tied to a report type (set on the [schedule of values](/docs/project-management/schedule-of-values)), ampOS fills in how many got done by counting the reports sent that week. That count can be off. Type the real number, and ampOS uses yours. Approved weeks become the bill, so make it right.

## Billing ahead

Need to bill ahead of the work? Add the extra in the month's last week. The customer only sees the month, not the weeks.

::: note
A week belongs to the month its Wednesday falls in. A week that crosses two months bills in one of them, never both.
:::

Every report in ampOS has print styling built in. What you get on paper is not a screenshot of the screen. It is a clean, formatted document with the app's controls stripped out.

## Printing one report

Open the report and use your browser's print command (**⌘P** or **Ctrl P**).

The print view removes dropdown arrows, number-input spinners, buttons, navigation, and everything else that only makes sense on screen. What is left is the data, laid out for paper.

## Saving as PDF

In the print dialog, choose **Save as PDF** as the destination instead of a printer. Same output, as a file.

## Print settings that matter

| Setting | Use |
|---|---|
| **Paper size** | Letter |
| **Margins** | Default |
| **Scale** | 100%. Do not "fit to page"; it shrinks the text. |
| **Background graphics** | On. Without it, table shading and headers disappear. |

::: warning
Background graphics off is the most common cause of "the PDF looks broken." Table headers and shaded rows are backgrounds. Turn it on.
:::

## Multiple reports at once

Do not print reports one by one and staple them. Build a **deliverable**. It combines every approved report into one PDF with a cover letter, an executive summary, and continuous page numbering.

See [Building a deliverable](/docs/deliverables/building-a-deliverable).

## Generated documents

Deliverable PDFs, proposals, purchase orders, and certificates are generated by the app rather than printed from a form. Open them from the job and print or download from the viewer.

Because they are generated from live data, do not edit the PDF and re-upload it. Fix the underlying data and generate again, or the next regeneration silently discards your edit.

## If a report prints badly

Work through these in order:

1. Background graphics are off. Turn them on.
2. Scale is not 100%.
3. You are printing from a browser other than Chrome or Edge. Try Chrome.
4. A very wide test grid is genuinely wider than the page. Try landscape.

If none of that fixes it, that report's print layout may have a real problem. Tell an administrator which report type and what went wrong.

## Page breaks

Reports are laid out so sections break sensibly. A test grid does not split with its header on one page and its rows on another. You do not need to insert breaks yourself.

## What the customer should get

The finished deliverable PDF, not individual report printouts. Individual printouts are for the field: a copy to hand to the plant electrician, or paper to work from when a tablet dies.

A job accumulates paper. ampOS keeps it in four different places, and knowing which is which saves a lot of hunting.

## Default files

When a job is created, ampOS copies your company's standard job documents onto it automatically: safety forms, JHA templates, standard checklists, whatever has been configured as a default.

You do not have to attach these by hand. If they are missing on a new job, an administrator needs to check the default file configuration.

## Attachments

Anything you upload to the job: customer drawings, the signed PO, the scope email, previous test data.

Upload from the job page. Files stay with the job permanently; closing or billing the job does not remove them.

::: tip
Attach the customer's one-line drawing at the start of the job. Every technician who opens the job then has it, instead of three of them texting the PM for it on separate days.
:::

## Pictures

Photos get their own tab because they are used differently from documents. Photos uploaded to the **Pictures** tab can be pulled directly into reports and into deliverables, rather than uploaded twice.

Take more than you think you need. Nameplates, as-found conditions, damage, and the panel with its cover off are all things you will wish you had photographed when a question comes up in six months.

## Generated documents

Documents ampOS produces itself: proposals, purchase orders, deliverable PDFs, certificates. These are generated from data in the app, so they are always current with what the app knows.

Open one from the job and it renders in the viewer. You can print or download from there.

Because they are generated, do not edit and re-upload them. Change the underlying data and generate again, or the next regeneration silently overwrites your edit.

## One-line drawings

Jobs can hold one-line drawings, which the engineering portal and the reports reference. These live in their own section because they get linked to from equipment records rather than just downloaded.

## Storage and size

Large files are fine. Full-resolution photos and multi-megabyte PDFs both work. Very large uploads over a phone hotspot in a substation will be slow; if you are in the field with bad signal, upload photos later from the office rather than blocking on it.

## Who can see what

Job files follow job permissions. Anyone who can open the job can see its files. There is no per-file permission.

::: warning
That means anything you attach to a job is visible to everyone with access to that job. Do not attach things that should not be: pricing you do not want the field crew debating, HR matters, personal information.
:::
